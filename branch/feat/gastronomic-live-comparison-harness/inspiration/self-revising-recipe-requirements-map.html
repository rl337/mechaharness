Self-revising recipe evolution requirements map

Maps themes from self-revising-recipe-evolution.md to stable backlog IDs (MH-SR-*), modules, and user stories.

Coverage: story means a persona story exercises an integrated library path; partial means helpers/schemas ship but auto-wiring or host integration remains open (see Gaps); gap means the product contract is not shipped. Inspiration status: proposal (source screenshot unverified); not an accepted requirement. Do not treat screenshot performance claims as benchmarks.

Ownership: MechaHarness owns recipe registry, typed composition, validation, deterministic execution, evaluation substrate, versioning/checkpoints, rollback hooks, observability, and safety gates. The AI assistant host owns workload selection, orchestration, scheduling, experiment budgets, evaluation campaigns, and promotion policy. home_network owns sandboxed workers and experiment stores. JuneSpark may supply inference for proposals/evals within local budgets.
